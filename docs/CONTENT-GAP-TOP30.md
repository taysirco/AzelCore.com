## Internal-linking architecture (how the authority compounds)

**Three-layer hub-and-spoke:**

1. **Money page** (commercial root) — `/car-insulation-jeddah`, `/building-glass-insulation`, `/paint-protection-film-jeddah`, dealer pages, `/calculator`. These are the conversion targets; everything points toward them.
2. **Pillar hubs** (the 3 new long-form pages, #1 / #2 / #3) sit *between* the money page and the spokes. Each pillar links **down** to all its spokes and **up** to the money page; every spoke links **up** to its pillar. The **decision pillar #2 (Tinting vs Ceramic vs PPF)** is the cross-cluster glue — it links to all three service money pages and is linked from each of them + `/calculator`.
3. **Spokes** (the ~25 articles) each link: **up** to the pillar + money page with a keyword-rich anchor, and **sideways** to 2–3 sibling spokes in the same cluster.

**Rules that make it rank:**
- **Every spoke is ≤2 clicks from a money page; zero orphans.** A spoke with no inbound internal link earns no authority.
- **Existing pages must add down-links to the new spokes** (this is the highest-leverage, most-skipped step). Examples baked into each entry above — e.g. the existing `tint-laws-saudi-2026` should link down to #4 (VLT-by-window) and #10 (fines/Fahas); `nano-ceramic-vs-carbon-vs-3m` → #7 and #14; the 10 district pages → #1 pillar + #5 cost; the 15 city pages → #3 pillar + #20 SBC compliance.
- **Anchor text:** money-page links use the exact-match primary keyword; spoke-to-spoke links use varied, descriptive anchors (avoid 30 identical anchors — Google reads that as manipulation). The pillar's down-links use each spoke's long-tail keyword.
- **Reciprocity within a cluster, not across:** keep cross-cluster links purposeful (decision pillar, cost, calculator) so each cluster stays topically tight.

## Publish sequencing (fastest authority compounding)

Build the **structure before the volume** — publish hubs first so later spokes have an authoritative parent to link to.

- **Phase 1 — Hubs & highest-demand (build first):** #1, #2, #3 (the three pillars), then #4 (VLT-by-window — the single most-searched KSA tint question), #5 (cost-by-car), #6 (PPF cost). Wire the money-page ⇄ pillar links immediately.
- **Phase 2 — Brand, PPF buildout, buyer education:** #7–#16. These convert and feed the dealer + PPF money pages; PPF was your thinnest cluster, so this closes the biggest gap.
- **Phase 3 — B2B / commercial & legal depth:** #17–#20, #26, #30. High-value building & fleet leads; SBC 601/602 (#20) establishes B2B/tender authority.
- **Phase 4 — Maintenance, health, long-tail:** #21–#25, #27–#29. Long-tail volume + engagement + YMYL trust (cite WHO / dermatology / SASO / MOI — no medical over-claims, consistent with the site's honesty standard).
- **After every publish:** add the down-links from the hub/pillar **and** 1–2 existing pages. The linking is what compounds — an unlinked article is wasted authority.
